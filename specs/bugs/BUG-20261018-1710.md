# BUG-20261018-1710: Recovery stops before the unlock channel is ready

## Summary

Bluetooth recovery waits for polling and can stop permanently before the phone
has an operational challenge subscription.

## Reproduction

Phone Bluetooth disabled at 17:09:33, enabled at 17:09:37 through ADB.
Watchdog woke at 17:09:48, connection established at 17:09:55, CCCD acknowledged
at 17:09:59: 22 seconds after radio enable. No STATE_ON recovery trigger.
Desktop previously rebuilt GATT during authentication upon discovering a dead
notification writer (16:51 and 16:59), disconnecting devices and delaying recovery.

## Expected

The stored bond survives disconnects. One service owns one GATT client per peer.
Radio enable triggers recovery immediately; setup remains bounded until CCCD
acknowledgement. Closed connections cannot mutate a newer attempt. Retries stop
while Bluetooth is off or the client is stopped.

## Root Cause

PersistentGattClient cancels its watchdog at STATE_CONNECTED. Discovery/CCCD
failures only log. Null connectGatt leaves no timer. No adapter-state receiver
resets the stale BluetoothGatt. Callbacks do not validate their handle, and
forceReconnect can revive a stopped client. Desktop stale notification detection
is deferred until an unlock request; its current recovery rebuilds the GATT app.

## Wire contract

Desktop peripheral, Android central, autoConnect=true, TRANSPORT_LE. Existing
per-bond UUIDs, nonce/HMAC/signature formats, LESC confirmation and per-unlock
biometrics stay in force. No SPEC 3.2 D1–D8 or 3.3 authentication changes.

## Acceptance

- JVM regressions for incomplete setup, stale callbacks, null opens, stop, radio cycle.
- Full Android tests/build and installed APK radio-cycle CCCD acknowledgement.
- Rust lint/test gates; dependency audit limitations recorded.
- Design report separates implemented changes from remaining architecture work.

## Fix and verification

`PersistentGattClient` keeps setup recovery armed until a successful CCCD write,
including null/throwing opens. Stopped clients cannot be revived by reconnect,
closed handles cannot deliver callbacks, and radio-off clients do not poll.
`SyauthCompanionService` observes adapter state and restarts its existing owned
clients immediately on STATE_ON.

All 116 Android JVM tests and the debug APK build pass. Installed APK radio cycle:
off 17:22:01, on/open 17:22:05.044, connected 17:22:11.091, CCCD acknowledged
17:22:13.016; daemon received subscription 17:22:13.119. Approximately 8 s versus
the baseline 22 s, one sample each. Subsequent challenge received at 17:23:26.
Bluetooth remains enabled and the active bond is retained.

Rust workspace tests pass: 461 passed, 8 ignored. Clippy with denied warnings
and formatting pass.
An additional full run exposed `lifecycle_smoke::refuses_second_instance`
timing out during shutdown: the fixture used the real peripheral, whose BlueZ
initialization occurs before shutdown polling. The smoke fixture now selects
the existing fake peripheral, retaining real process/lock/signal coverage without
competing with the desktop's Bluetooth owner. The focused lifecycle tests pass.
The full workspace rerun also passes after that fixture correction.
The lint pipeline still fails cargo-deny on the existing unmaintained
zbus_names / zvariant / zvariant_derive advisories RUSTSEC-2026-0371,
RUSTSEC-2026-0370, and RUSTSEC-2026-0372. Dependencies were not changed.
The scope check used equivalent `rg` filtering rather than the Makefile's
git-based recipe, following the no-git instruction.
